Why Edmonton

Edmonton is a dynamic, diverse, and resilient city at the heart of Canada. We're a hub for innovation & opportunity with room to grow.

  • ✓ 1.1M+ population within Edmonton city limits
  • ✓ Robust infrastructure, transportation, and logistics hub
